  Total Faults = 10!
  Increase: 9 faults -> 10 faults despite a 33% increase in memory frames!

Why LRU Cannot Exhibit Belady's Anomaly (Stack Algorithm Theorem):
  Let B(m, t) be the set of pages in memory of size m at time t.
  For an algorithm to be a stack algorithm: B(m, t) must be a subset of B(m+1, t) for all t.
  LRU simply chooses the top m elements of the reference stack.
  Therefore: B_LRU(m, t) is strictly contained in B_LRU(m+1, t).
  A page fault occurs if and only if the requested page is not in B(m, t).
  Since B(m, t) is a subset of B(m+1, t), any hit with m frames is guaranteed to hit
  with m+1 frames. Hence faults NEVER increase as frames increase.
