5. HOSPITAL RECORDS SERVER STORAGE RECOMMENDATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommendation: **Tiered Architecture with RAID 10 for Database and RAID 6 for Imaging Archives**.
1. **Tier 1 (EHR Transactional Database & OS): RAID 10**
   - No parity write penalty allows thousands of IOPS for patient record updates.
   - 24-hour rebuild time does not stress remaining drives, preventing second-drive collapse.
2. **Tier 2 (PACS Medical Imaging Archive - CT/MRI): RAID 6**
   - High usable capacity (16 TB usable out of 24 TB raw).
   - Dual parity protects patient scans against double-disk failure during rebuilds.
3. **Swap Strategy:**
   - 64 GB dedicated swap partition striped across high-speed NVMe drives to prevent
     paging latency from freezing emergency room workstations during peak query bursts.
