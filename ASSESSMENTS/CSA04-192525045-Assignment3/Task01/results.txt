7. CONCLUSION & RECOMMENDATION [THEORETICAL RESULT]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommendation for Banking Software:
- Use **Dynamic Shared Libraries (Load-Time Linking)** for standard enterprise modules
  to maximize memory deduplication and minimize network bandwidth during updates (99% savings).
- Use **Runtime Loading (`dlopen`)** for specialized security, regulatory, or hardware modules
  to achieve fault isolation (so that missing optional hardware or regional tax plugins
  never prevent the teller terminal from starting) and hot-plug capability.
